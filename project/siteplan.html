Site Plan

Site Name

MonteviHair

I chose this name because it is a mix between the word Montevideo, which is my city, and the word Hair. This page will have hair related stores in Montevideo, so the name combines those 2 things.

Site Purpose

This website has the purpose of showcasing many hair related stores on Montevideo, including Hair Salons and Barber Shops. It represents an association where stores can publish their info, and customers can access them and make a schedule through the page.

Scenarios

  • How often do people need a hair saloon or a barber shop?
  • How many stores should I include?
